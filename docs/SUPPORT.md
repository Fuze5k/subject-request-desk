# Subject Request Desk: support

Support is provided by the developer, Yaroslav Hryhorenko.

- **Contact:** GitHub Issues of the public repository (link below).
- **Hours:** weekdays, replies within 2 business days (Ukraine time, UTC+2/+3).
- **Bugs and requests:** open an issue in the public repository: https://github.com/Fuze5k/subject-request-desk/issues.
- **Security issues:** open an issue titled "security" without technical details, or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the repository. Please do not post details publicly before there is a fix.

When you write, please include: your site name, the product (Jira or Confluence), what you did, what you expected, what happened. Do not send real personal data about data subjects.
